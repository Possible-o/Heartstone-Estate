Ask the Lagos office

Do I need an agent to become a homeowner?

No. Search and property details are public. Sign in to save homes and submit or manage your personal inspection requests.

Is an inspection request a confirmed viewing?

No. Your preferred date is a request. The agent must confirm the property's availability and agree an appointment with you.

How are rental prices shown?

Each home lists its rental price and payment period. Contact the letting team for availability, tenancy terms and any additional costs.

Can the office help me sell or let?

Yes. Our Lagos team offers sales, letting and valuation services. Contact the team to discuss your property.
